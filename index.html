Local-first by design

More personal than a fixed course, more private than a SaaS

Most vocabulary tools ask you to leave the page, create an account, pay a subscription, or hand over your browsing data. Vocab takes a different position: everything stays in your browser.

  • No backend server — your words never leave your machine
  • No account, no login, no email required
  • No telemetry or tracking of any kind
  • AI calls go straight from your browser to your provider

Your data is yours

Stored in IndexedDB on your device. Export anytime as plain JSON. Delete in one click.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Vocab?

A local-first Chrome extension that saves words while you browse, highlights them everywhere you go, and explains them with your own AI provider key.

Do I need an account?

No. Vocab has no backend and no account. You add your own AI provider key in the options page, and all data is stored locally in your browser.

Which AI providers are supported?

OpenAI, OpenRouter, Google Gemini, Anthropic, Ollama, and LM Studio. You choose the model and control every request.

Is my browsing data tracked?

No. There is no telemetry. Saved words, notes, and settings live in your browser's IndexedDB and are never uploaded anywhere except directly to the AI provider you configure.

Does it work on bilingual pages?

Yes. Vocab highlights saved words in both columns of original/translation layouts, so passive recall works even when you read side by side.

Can I move my vocabulary to another device?

Yes. Export your library as versioned JSON and import it elsewhere, with merge or replace options.
